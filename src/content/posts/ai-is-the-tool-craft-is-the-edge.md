There is an art and science to building good products. That was true when every line was typed by hand, and it is still true now that a model can write the first draft of almost anything.

Most AI content lands at one of two extremes: shallow "copy this prompt" hype, or income claims nobody can check. Neither helps someone who actually has to ship something people want.

## What this is

Craft with Intent is three builders, with design, engineering and product leadership between us, putting that craft to work in public. We are trying to build delightful, useful, reliable products with AI, and to find out which of them can become a sustainable business or a standout career.

Between the three of us, we have more than sixty years of experience shipping consumer apps, games, and AI platforms. Instead of keeping our workflows inside private chat tabs, we publish every teardown, workbench, and failed hypothesis on one shared site.

## The four gates

Every project we document walks through the same four gates: problem discovery, a middle-path MVP with one intentional delight, engineering trade-offs, and shipped code with real distribution. Each gate ends with the same honest question: how does this make money?

When AI hits a craft ceiling, novices re-prompt the model harder. Experienced builders stop prompting final outputs and build the missing workbenches, solvers, and verification harnesses around the AI.

## Practising what we preach

Even this site went through Gate 3 before a line of template markup shipped. We rejected heavy dynamic stacks in favor of a pre-built static publication hosted on edge infrastructure so fixed monthly hosting stays at zero.

## What we will and won't tell you

We will show real code, real screenshots, and real costs. We will credit work accurately: AskQuip is Andy and Mike's, and it earns modest, real revenue, nothing more. And we will share exact revenue for new bets only once they show real signal, so a lucky week never reads like a business.

Join us as we figure out how to make money with AI. Some of this will work. We will show you the parts that don't.
