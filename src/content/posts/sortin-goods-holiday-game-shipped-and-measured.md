Shipping fast is easy to fake on localhost. Shipping a complete mobile puzzle game across 177 Google Play countries and the iOS App Store, with verified deep links, 11 localized listings, and real multiplayer speed duels, forces clean product cuts immediately.

## Designing for thumb zones and instant clarity

Instead of cramming store screenshots with feature badges or price buttons, we tightened the core sorting shelf loop to fill portrait mobile screens cleanly and tested real human visual hierarchy on physical phones.

## Measuring distribution honestly

Live telemetry keeps us honest after approval. Rather than celebrating vanity impressions, every short-form video creative and portal slice is graded against real store page views, installs, and retention—and weak hooks get cut quickly.
